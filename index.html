Course Terms & Policies课程条款与规则

1. Scope & Booking

These Course Terms & Policies apply to live lessons and lesson packages purchased directly from Teacher Xiāo. A booking becomes confirmed when the lesson format, duration, schedule and price have been agreed and, where required, payment has been received.

2. Pricing & Payment

Prices shown on the website are the prices applicable to the selected package at the time of purchase. Package discounts apply to the package as a whole and are not retroactively applied to individual lessons if the package is later cancelled, except where mandatory consumer rights require otherwise.

For group lessons, payment is due after the group and schedule have been confirmed, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

3. 1-on-1 Rescheduling

24+ hours before class: the full lesson may be rescheduled at no additional cost.

12–24 hours before class: the lesson fee is non-refundable, but 50% of the scheduled lesson time may be rescheduled.

Less than 12 hours before class: the lesson fee is non-refundable and the lesson cannot be rescheduled.

4. Group Lessons

Students may report an absence at any time. The full lesson fee remains payable, and the missed group lesson cannot be rescheduled individually. A class recording will be provided after the lesson when a recording is available.

5. Teacher Rescheduling

The teacher will provide at least 12 hours' notice of a reschedule whenever reasonably possible. If the teacher needs to reschedule with less than 12 hours' notice, one complimentary lesson will normally be provided, subject to exceptional circumstances and applicable consumer rights.

6. Package Cancellation & Refunds

If a package is cancelled after lessons have started, completed lessons will normally be recalculated using the standard single-lesson rate applicable to the purchased format and lesson duration. The remaining package balance will then be refunded, subject to applicable law.

Example: a 20 × 60-minute 1-on-1 package costs €440. If 5 lessons have been completed, those 5 lessons are recalculated at the €27 standard rate (€135), leaving €305 to be refunded, subject to any mandatory statutory rights.

7. Free Trial

The 30-minute trial lesson is free. A trial does not automatically create a recurring booking or paid package. Any future lessons require a separate confirmation.

8. Attendance, Late Arrival & No-Show

Students are expected to join on time and from a suitable learning environment. If a student arrives late, the lesson normally ends at the originally scheduled time. If a student does not attend, the applicable cancellation policy above applies.

9. Technical Problems

Students are responsible for having a suitable device, stable internet connection and access to the agreed teaching platform. If a technical problem on the teacher's side prevents the lesson from taking place, the lesson will be rescheduled or otherwise resolved at no additional cost. If the problem is on the student's side, the applicable lesson and cancellation rules remain in effect, except where the parties agree otherwise.

10. Platforms & Class Materials

Lessons may be delivered through ClassIn, Zoom, Preply, or another agreed platform. Teaching materials supplied by the teacher are for the student's personal educational use and may not be redistributed, resold or publicly uploaded without permission.

11. Class Recordings

Where a group recording is provided, it is intended for the enrolled students' personal learning use only. Recordings must not be shared publicly, sold, redistributed or used for commercial purposes.

12. Respectful Learning Environment

Students and the teacher are expected to communicate respectfully. Harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats, abusive behaviour or repeated disruption may result in the lesson or course being ended. Any refund or remaining balance will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the case.

13. Students Under 18

For students under 18, a parent or legal guardian must make the booking or provide the required consent. Parents or guardians remain responsible for the student's participation and contact information.

14. Consumer Rights

Nothing in these terms limits mandatory rights under applicable consumer protection law. For online service contracts in Spain, statutory withdrawal rights may apply, subject to legal exceptions and the conditions applicable when a service begins during the withdrawal period. Where mandatory law applies, it takes priority over these terms.

15. Privacy & Personal Information

Information submitted through booking, availability and payment forms is used to arrange lessons, communicate with students, process payments and provide the requested service. A separate Privacy Notice should be provided on the website before launch and will govern the processing of personal data.

16. Changes & Exceptional Circumstances

Course schedules, platforms or lesson arrangements may occasionally need to change because of circumstances outside reasonable control. Where this happens, the teacher will communicate with the student and seek a reasonable solution. Nothing in this section limits mandatory legal rights.

Before payment, the student must actively confirm that they have read and agree to these Course Terms & Policies. A copy of the applicable terms and purchase confirmation should be retained by both parties.
