1. Scope & Booking

Prices on Preply may differ from the direct prices shown on this website because Preply has its own platform pricing, fees and commission structure. Preply bookings are subject to the terms and payment rules of Preply.

3. 1-on-1 Cancellations & Rescheduling

This section applies when a student wants to cancel, skip, or change the date or start time of a confirmed 1-on-1 lesson. Each cancellation or rescheduling request counts toward the student's monthly 1-on-1 cancellation allowance. The allowance is intended to give students reasonable flexibility while keeping a regular reserved time for the teacher. It does not create a separate package extension.

Monthly cancellation allowance: 1 lesson per month for students studying once a week, 2 lessons per month for twice a week, 3 lessons per month for three times a week, and 4 lessons per month for four times a week. For a custom weekly frequency, the allowance normally equals the agreed weekly frequency. Unused cancellation allowance does not carry over to the next month.

24+ hours before class: one cancellation or rescheduling request within the monthly allowance may be moved to another available time at no additional cost.

12 to 24 hours before class: if the request is still within the monthly allowance, 50% of the scheduled lesson time may be rescheduled. For example, a 60-minute lesson may be changed to a 30-minute lesson at a later time. The remaining 50% of the scheduled lesson time is treated as used and is not refundable.

Less than 12 hours before class: the lesson fee is non-refundable and the lesson cannot be rescheduled, even if the monthly allowance has not been used.

After the monthly allowance has been used: any additional cancellation or skipped lesson is charged in full and cannot be refunded or rescheduled, regardless of the notice given, except where mandatory law requires otherwise.

Rescheduling is always subject to the teacher's availability. If a student needs to change a lesson, the request should be made as early as possible.

4. Group Lessons

Students may report an absence from an individual group lesson at any time. An absence does not cancel the student's group package. The full lesson fee remains payable, the missed lesson cannot be rescheduled individually, and a class recording will be provided after the lesson when a recording is available.

Stopping a group package: if a student wants to cancel the remaining group package, the cancellation takes effect on the date the cancellation request is received. All group lessons that took place before that date are treated as used, whether the student attended or was absent. Previously missed lessons remain non-refundable, including where a recording was provided. Future lessons after the effective cancellation date are treated as unused and may be refunded according to Section 6, subject to applicable law.

5. Teacher Rescheduling

The teacher will provide at least 12 hours' notice of a reschedule whenever reasonably possible. If the teacher needs to reschedule with less than 12 hours' notice, one complimentary lesson will normally be provided, subject to exceptional circumstances and applicable consumer rights.

6. Package Cancellation & Refunds

If a package is cancelled after lessons have started, lessons already delivered are charged using the package discount tier that corresponds to the number of lessons already used. The remaining unused balance is then refunded, subject to applicable law.

The calculation works as follows: 1 to 9 used lessons are charged at the 5-lesson package rate; 10 to 19 used lessons are charged at the 10-lesson package rate; 20 or more used lessons are charged at the 20-lesson package rate. The rate is applied to all used lessons, not split into separate blocks.

For group packages, a lesson counts as used once the class has taken place, whether or not the student attended. A missed lesson is therefore included in the used-lesson calculation and is not refundable. If a recording was provided, the lesson is also treated as delivered.

Example: a 20 × 60-minute 1-on-1 package costs €440. If 11 lessons have been used, all 11 lessons are recalculated at the 10-lesson package rate of €25 per lesson, for a total of €275. The remaining €165 is refunded, subject to applicable law.

Refunds are normally returned through the original payment method. The time required for the refund to appear may depend on the payment provider.
